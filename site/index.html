How it works

  1. Your question is matched to entries in a small knowledge base of Indian laws, schemes and helplines – each checked on the official government page.
  2. An AI model writes a simple answer using only those entries, in your language.
  3. A code guard blocks any section number, helpline, number or website that is not in the knowledge base.
  4. Words about violence, threats or suicide skip the AI and show emergency numbers at once.

Try to break it

Privacy – NyayaSaathi does not save your questions or ask for your name. Your language choice is kept only in your own browser.
